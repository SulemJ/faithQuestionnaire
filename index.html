KEEP THE LAMPS LIT. LIVE WITH GREATER COHERENCE AND FAITHFULNESS.

This questionnaire is the starting point for your work within The I AM Project — Faith-Based Cultural Neurological Alignment Profile (CNAP). It is designed to help us understand how coherently your beliefs, habits, relationships, and daily practices are aligned with the life you profess to value.

The assessment explores seven interconnected domains of faithful living:Wellness, Spirituality, Family, Relationships, Career, Finances, and Contribution. Together, they reveal where life feels grounded, intentional, and obedient — and where drift, inconsistency, or fragmentation may be present.

This is not simply about ideals. It is about the lived reality of your life over the past 30–90 days. For each question, choose the response that most accurately reflects what is actually occurring, and answer honestly rather than aspirationally.

Seven Areas of
Faithful Living

  • Wellness — Stewardship of the body
  • Spirituality — Worship and devotion
  • Family — Order and care
  • Relationships — Love and reconciliation
  • Career — Vocation and diligence
  • Finances — Stewardship and generosity
  • Contribution — Service and witness

The Five Levels

A-1 is information, not condemnation. A-5 is responsibility, not completion.
1— Disconnected : Limited intentional attention; largely reactive, neglected, or unstable.
2— Awakening : Recognizing importance and beginning to engage, but behaviour remains inconsistent.
3— Practicing : Meaningful behaviours and disciplines in place, becoming increasingly reliable.
4— Integrated : A stable part of how you live, generally present across changing circumstances.
5— Generative : Deeply integrated and increasingly creating benefit beyond you through presence, service, and influence.

Every area of life requires continued tending.
